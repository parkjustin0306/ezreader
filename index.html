ez reader

a browser extension for better reading accessibility

ez reader is a simple yet powerful browser extension designed to make any webpage more comfortable to read. we believe that everyone should have control over how they experience written content on the web.

with just a click, users can instantly adjust text size and switch between different fonts to find what works best for their eyes. whether you have dyslexia, visual challenges, or simply prefer a specific reading style, ez reader puts you in control of your web reading experience.

no complex settings, no overwhelming options - just simple, effective tools that make reading easier. we're focused on the essentials: font selection and text sizing that work seamlessly across any website you visit.

privacy policy

clear, honest, and actually readable

Key Points

  • We process your reading preferences locally in your browser - no data is sent to external servers
  • Your reading settings are never stored on our servers
  • We prioritize your privacy and never sell, rent, or store your data beyond what is necessary
  • All font and text adjustments happen directly in your browser
  • We don't collect any personal information or browsing history

Introduction

Welcome to EZ Reader ("we," "our," or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our browser extension.

Information We Collect

When you use EZ Reader to customize your reading experience, we process:

  • Reading Preferences: Your chosen font, text size, color settings, and other accessibility adjustments
  • Extension Settings: Your personal configuration for the extension's behavior

We do not collect or store any personal information, browsing history, or website content unless explicitly provided by you for purposes such as contacting support.

How We Use Your Information

The primary use of your reading preferences is to help you read web content more comfortably. Specifically:

  • Your reading settings are processed locally in your browser
  • Font changes and text adjustments happen directly on your device
  • No reading data or website content is transmitted to external servers
  • Your preferences are stored locally in your browser for future sessions

Data Retention and Security

Since all processing happens locally in your browser:

  • Your reading preferences remain on your device
  • No personal information or browsing data is stored on our servers
  • Your settings persist in your browser's local storage for convenience
  • You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings

We implement industry-standard security practices to ensure your data remains private and secure during your browsing sessions.

Permissions

EZ Reader requires access to web pages solely to apply readability enhancements and text customization features. We do not use this access to collect, monitor, or transmit your browsing data.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us at:
